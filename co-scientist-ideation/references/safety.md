# Safety, Provenance, and Human Control

## Dual gate

1. Review the research goal, authorization, privacy, and evidence envelope before retrieval or delegation.
2. Review every candidate before it enters Review, comparison, Evolution, or final presentation.

Decomposing a prohibited task across perspectives does not make it permissible. Offer bounded
non-operational risk assessment, detection, governance, or defensive research when appropriate.

## Candidate-local versus whole-run scope

Reject only the affected candidate when its mechanism, intervention, proposed use, citation, or
feasibility is unsafe, unethical, fabricated, or unsupported and the problem is not shared. Prohibit
that object's downstream use while preserving valid siblings. A local fabricated citation also
triggers a bounded check of the shared evidence map.

Stop the whole run for an unsafe or prohibited goal; an authorization, privacy, or privileged-
information breach; leakage of unavailable solution material or prior outputs; material goal drift;
fabrication or contamination in shared evidence; no usable evidence/observation anchor; or a missing
human decision that changes safety or authorization.

## Privacy and unpublished work

- Record whether material is public, embargoed, unpublished, proprietary, identifiable, or sensitive.
- Default to excluding unpublished hypotheses, exact private values, data, and collaborator details
  from web queries. Use abstracted terms or user-supplied public references.
- Do not upload local files or data to an external service without explicit permission.
- Subagents inherit the same sandbox and permission boundary; failure never broadens authority.
- Keep ordinary work in chat. Persist only when the user requests a durable artifact.

## Evidence and citation discipline

- Verify source identity and that the actual content bears on the attached claim. A search snippet is
  discovery material, not claim evidence.
- Apply the quantitative/citation gate in `contracts.md` to exact quantities and named-source
  comparisons. Model memory, project memory, prior chat, and agent agreement may guide retrieval but
  cannot supply an admitted scientific fact without a current source/location binding.
- Prefer primary sources and exact identifiers; keep source location and limitation visible.
- Preserve contradictory, limiting, negative, null, unavailable, reported, and internal evidence.
- Distinguish fact, interpretation, hypothesis, prediction, and design choice.
- Repeated claims from one source family are not independent support.
- Corpus absence and empty search are not novelty evidence; keep novelty unverified when coverage is insufficient.
- An indirect mechanistic bridge remains an assumption even when several perspectives agree.

## Evaluation discipline

- Temporary subagents may share model biases. Describe them as separated perspectives, not independent
  domain experts.
- Agent agreement, debate, comparison, Meta-review, or structural validation does not establish truth,
  novelty, peer review, replication, or experimental validation.
- Blind decision-relevant comparisons from author identity and current preference; allow ties,
  reject-both, insufficient evidence, and order instability.
- Preserve minority objections that could change the decision.
- Do not request, expose, or store private chain-of-thought; retain concise rationales and evidence.

## Research-integrity checks

Apply only checks relevant to the live claim. For empirical work, examine provenance, sampling and
independent unit, leakage/duplicates, baseline fairness, uncertainty, multiple comparisons, resources,
and whether the metric tests the claim. For computational, physical, or biological work, also examine
dimensions/units, mechanism, controls, boundary conditions, independent experimental units, and the
line between simulation and empirical evidence. Formal claims require an explicit calculation,
residual, or counterexample in independent Review.

## Human and execution control

- The scientist defines or confirms the goal and may critique or remove any candidate.
- The scientist owns final selection and whether missing evidence is worth acquiring.
- Block 6 ends with a human decision; it does not automatically begin a new pass.
- **Execution is not authorized by this Skill output.** Paid services, downloads, code, GPU training,
  data collection, wet-lab work, repository mutation, external messages, and paper writing require a
  separate explicit request and the appropriate owner.
